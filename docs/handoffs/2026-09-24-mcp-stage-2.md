snapshots, bounded probes, normalized inventories and documented uncertainty.

  history coordinates; snapshots dated 2025 do not prove current coverage.

  expected years (2045–2054 and 2085–2094). Membership is not weather validation;
  baseline archive membership remains documentation-only.

  analyzed. Strict automatic matching leaves 61 U.S. products unknown. The owner
  accepted explicit annotations for all 61: 56 Hawaiian metadata correspondences
  (same complete filenames, different WMO region directory), three approximate
  localities and two name/code conflicts. Do not describe all 61 as unidentified,
  nor treat all 61 as exact verified station coordinates. No archive-byte or EPW
  header equivalence was tested.
- CMIP6: 636 coherent historical/scenario seven-variable model/member/grid
  combinations in a dated catalog; complete time-window validation and per-model
  licensing remain separate checks.
- PVGIS/NSRDB general footprints, moving availability endpoints and source reuse
  terms remain explicit limitations rather than fabricated coverage.

The annotation layer preserves original automatic evidence. It requires the
original catalog and source snapshots to match pinned checksums and parse
successfully; otherwise acceptance becomes stale/unresolved. Approximate locations
must not become exact station points, and coordinate agreement must not deduplicate
weather requests without scientific equivalence.
