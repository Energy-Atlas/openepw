# MCP Stage 1 — availability investigation execution plan

Owner approved for implementation on 2026-09-23. Branch: `feature/mcp`.
Spec: [ADR 0003](../../decisions/0003-mcp-availability-and-batches.md).
The approved conversation plan is implemented by the bounded research stages below;
no production discovery, MCP, planning or job behavior changes are authorized here.

## Deliverables and execution order

1. Build a separate research CLI with offline `plan`, `analyze`, `report` and explicit
   `collect --only`. Keep snapshots under ignored `.local/mcp-availability/` and
   preserve existing probe scripts/evidence.
2. Test the actual HTTP boundary with synthetic transports: resume, reservations,
   budgets, redirects, rate limits, deadlines, secret exclusion and Range behavior.
3. Collect authoritative inventories/docs for all six weather access providers,
   Pangeo CMIP6 and OEDI WRF. Analyze complete downloaded catalogs locally. Select
   location/year probes only where they answer an unresolved question.
4. Normalize station/product/period/future metadata without claiming weather
   completeness. Keep adapter support separate from broader source capabilities.
5. Publish sanitized evidence, an availability matrix, source register, bounded
   probe ledger, exact limitations and Stage 2 catalog/refresh proposal.
6. Verify offline tests/lint/docs and commit tooling separately from findings.

## Binding ceilings

- 60 metadata/document/catalog requests; 12 location/date-specific probes overall.
- 200 MB total body bytes; 5 MB ordinary response; 100 MB Pangeo CSV exception;
  10 MB aggregate directory reads per OEDI scenario archive.
- One serial request; at least two seconds between starts on the same host;
  30-second transport timeout and 60-second per-request deadline. No automatic retries.
- Count failed calls/redirects. Persist attempt and byte reservations before I/O.
  Interrupted reservations remain charged conservatively.
- Provider probes: Open-Meteo 3, PVGIS 2, NOAA 2, NSRDB 3. OneBuilding at most
  12 pages and two link levels; CMIP6 at most four selected Zarr metadata documents.
- Stop on rate limits/ignored Range/budget exhaustion; unknown is a valid outcome.
  No credentials/accounts, queued CDS jobs, climate-variable chunks, weather ZIP
  members, exhaustive spatial/year sweeps or automatic limit expansion.

## Evidence semantics and acceptance

Separate eligibility (supported/excluded/unknown) from documentation, inventory or
targeted-probe evidence. Preserve original temporal/spatial representations and
conflicts. Station operating periods do not prove continuous hourly data. TMY source
periods are not actual-year availability; sampled CMIP metadata cannot validate
other stores. Raw bodies stay local; tracked reports exclude hourly weather and
unreviewed third-party inventories.

Show requested-location occurrences separately from shared source identities in a
local mapping example. Do not change production output identity or export behavior.
Previous weather runs/QC summaries are excluded; advanced reuse stays future work.

Completion requires every source to have a defensible catalog strategy or explicit
limitation, reproducible offline analysis, bounded live evidence and a Stage 2
handoff. It does not require comprehensive live coverage or production integration.

## Accepted OneBuilding follow-up plan — 2026-09-23

The owner accepted the following coordinate-discovery approach. Record the plan
now; this decision does not claim completed matching/geocoding or approve Stage 2.

1. Parse published catalog station identifiers and location labels locally. Match
   identifiers to the downloaded NOAA history where the identifier convention is
   established and country/name evidence agrees. Preserve ambiguous and unmatched
   entries; do not assume every numeric filename token is a NOAA station ID.
2. For unmatched products, investigate an authoritative place index or a published
   source map/index, checking access and metadata reuse terms. Evaluate a small
   representative sample before broader geocoding; no exhaustive API calls.
3. Store approximate place coordinates separately from source-station coordinates,
   including provenance, matching method, precision (airport/town/region), and
   uncertainty. Name-derived locations must never silently become verified EPW
   coordinates. A station-ID match is inventory evidence, not EPW-header verification.
4. Use approximate coordinates for regional candidate discovery only. Verify native
   coordinates before precise distance/elevation recommendations. Retain each
   product identity and TMY reference period when multiple products share a place.
5. Validate with synthetic ambiguous-name/identifier cases and document sample
   matching outcomes and unresolved entries. Keep full third-party indexes local
   unless redistribution permission is established.

Acceptance of the OneBuilding approach did not itself authorize additional OEDI
requests. The subsequent OEDI decision is recorded below.

## Follow-up execution results — 2026-09-23

The agreed plan commits were already present (882e0ea and 58b560a) before execution.
Work remains on feature/mcp and outside production discovery/MCP code.

- OEDI: both one-time directory revisions succeeded, preserving saved ETags and
  exact byte ranges. Parsed 47,361 entries per archive: 47,360 EPW filenames and a
  directory entry. Both scenarios contain all 2,368 published sites and all 20
  expected future years for each. No duplicates/unparsed EPW names or unmatched
  sites. Membership is established; weather content and baseline remain untested.
- OneBuilding: source pages already link coordinate KMLs and XLSX indexes. Three
  KML attempts were rejected on declared size before body consumption. The U.S.
  and European XLSX alternatives succeeded within the ordinary response limit.
  All 12 allowed OneBuilding metadata requests are now used; no more were made.
- Ruling: use exact product URLs in the provider's published coordinate indexes
  ahead of inferred NOAA coordinates. This is stronger direct evidence than place
  geocoding. Keep both evidence candidates and flag numerical disagreements;
  coordinates still require native EPW verification for precise recommendations.
  Cost if the index is inaccurate: regional candidate distances can be inaccurate,
  so the evidence remains explicitly unverified at the EPW level.
- NOAA matching requires a six-character catalog identifier, country agreement
  (NOAA FIPS codes) and a meaningful shared name token with a unique candidate.
  Local results: U.S. 13,355 published-index + 1,543 NOAA matches, 1,572 unresolved;
  U.K. 1,451 published-index matches, zero unresolved; Australia 3,505 NOAA matches,
  227 unresolved. These are products, not unique stations.
- Ruling: defer approximate place geocoding for unresolved products. The published
  coordinate indexes resolved the selected sample more directly, and the provider
  request ceiling is reached. No guessed place coordinates or unbounded geocoder
  requests are justified. Cost: the unresolved subset stays unknown for now.
- Source copyright notice does not establish redistribution permission for full
  indexes. Full spreadsheets, normalized coordinate joins and ZIP directories stay
  ignored/local; only summary findings and selected factual observations are tracked.
- Added failing-then-passing tests for resumed OEDI allowance, conservative country/
  name/identifier joins, coordinate conflicts, workbook parsing and missing archive
  years/sites. Independent review found cross-source coordinate evidence could be
  hidden by the preferred published point; retained both with a disagreement flag.
  Also treated malformed inline strings and negative shared-string references as
  material parsing issues (crash/misread risk), reproduced each and fixed them.
- Overall ledger: 38 metadata attempts, 3 probes, 138,913,341 application bytes;
  no additional weather probes. OEDI charges are 11,816,413 / 11,816,677 bytes of
  17 MB each. Remaining total allowance: 61,086,659 bytes.

Verification: 35 focused research tests passed; full suite 136 passed, 14 opt-in
live tests skipped, two dependency deprecation warnings. Targeted Ruff and offline
